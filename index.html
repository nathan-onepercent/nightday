Our mission

College doesn't run on one schedule. Neither should a wellness kit.

Day/Night is a premium wellness and safety brand, built for incoming college freshmen around one product: the Survive & Thrive Kit.

Our mission is to balance the duality of the college experience. The Night half keeps students safe and protected during their social outings — knowing what's in a drink, having a way to call for help, getting home safe. The Day half covers the supplements and hydration it takes to wake up productive, healthy, and focused, so a late night doesn't cost the whole next day.

The kit ships on a subscription, drop by drop, and it's built for two people at once: the student who uses it, and the parent who's often the one setting it up. Formulas are separate for women and men, because the science is too — and one less thing to manage is the whole point.
